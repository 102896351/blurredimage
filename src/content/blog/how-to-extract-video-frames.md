- **Use High-Quality Source Video**: The quality of the extracted image depends entirely on the source video.

- **Avoid Motion Blur**: Try to pick frames where the subject is relatively still or the shutter speed was high.

- **Check Lighting**: Well-lit scenes produce sharper, less grainy still images.
