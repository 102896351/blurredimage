## Censor Photo Online: A Practical Way to Redact What You Share

When a photo carries a face you didn't ask permission to post, a number plate, a name or a screenshot full of account details, the responsible move is to **censor the photo** before it goes anywhere. You don't need desktop software or a paid app — you can censor a photo online in a few seconds, right in your browser.

## What "Censoring" Actually Means

Censoring (or redacting) an image means making specific parts unreadable while leaving the rest intact. Common redaction targets:

- **Faces** of bystanders, clients or strangers in the background
- **Licence plates** on cars, vans and delivery vehicles
- **Text** such as names, emails, order numbers and addresses in screenshots
- **Account details** visible in app captures and chat screenshots
- **Documents** where only one field needs to stay visible

For permanent hiding, a solid cover block beats a soft blur. For short strings like names or plates, pixelation is a solid middle ground.

## How to Censor a Photo Online

A simple workflow that works on any device:

1. **Open the image** in a browser-based editor such as [Blur Image Online](/blur-image).
2. **Select the area** with the brush, rectangle or lasso tool — draw over the face, plate or text you want gone.
3. **Apply the effect** — Gaussian blur, pixelation or a solid cover, depending on how permanent the hiding should be.
4. **Export only the redacted copy** — the original stays on your device.

For group photos and events, use [Blur Face Online](/blur-face) to hide every face in one pass. For screenshots with sensitive fields, [Blur Text in Image](/blur-text) targets the words directly. For vehicles, [Blur Licence Plate Online](/blur-license-plate) hides the plate in seconds.

## Censor vs Blur vs Pixelate

| Effect | Best for | Permanence |
|---|---|---|
| Solid cover | Account numbers, full documents | Highest |
| Pixelation | Short strings: names, plates | High |
| Gaussian blur | Faces, backgrounds | Medium-High |

If you're unsure how strong to go, [Automatic Privacy Blur](/privacy-blur) can detect candidate faces, plates and text areas so you can confirm what to hide.

## A Quick Redaction Checklist

Before you post, ask:

- Is there a face that isn't the intended subject? → censor it
- Does a plate or vehicle appear? → redact it
- Are names, emails or numbers visible? → cover them
- Is an address or location readable? → hide it
- Could any detail identify a person indirectly? → censor to be safe

## FAQ

### Can I censor a photo on mobile?
Yes. Browser-based tools run on Android and iOS with no app install, so you can redact on the device you took the photo with.

### Does censoring change the original file?
No. Redaction happens locally in your browser; you download a processed copy and the original never leaves your device as part of the normal workflow.

### Is pixelation or blur better for faces?
For everyday sharing, a strong blur or pixelation hides faces well. For highly sensitive records, prefer a solid cover block.
